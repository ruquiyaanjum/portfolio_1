Our story

From a village oven to Electronic City.

Raja is 22, and he has already spent 9 years baking. He learned the work in his village, carried it to Gulbarga, then to Bangalore, and then opened this bakery on his own.

  1. 6 years

    In his village

    He ran everything alone: the mixing, the baking, the finishing and the counter.

  2. 1 year

    Gulbarga

    A year at a local bakery in the city.

  3. 2 years

    Bangalore

    Two years baking at Brio Bakehouse.

  4. Today

    The Patel's Oven

    His own bakery, started by himself, with FSSAI registration in place.

5 / 5

That is how the people he has baked for rate his work. Clean, tasty, and made dil se, from the heart.

He came from a village and started this alone. Your support is what keeps this oven on. Thank you.
